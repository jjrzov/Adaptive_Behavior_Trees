\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}
    The choice between the constructions could be made before either tree is built.
    For each sibling of a \texttt{GoalSequence}, relevance analysis over the action database over-approximates the actions and literals its subtree could contain, starting from the sibling's goal literals and adding the actions that achieve or require them until nothing new is added.
    If no action relevant to one sibling deletes a literal relevant to another, the unified tree is expected to cover every state DNF compilation covers.
    Where the condition fails, the interacting siblings could be merged back into a single condition regressed jointly while the independent ones stay factored.
    Proving that the condition suffices and evaluating the merged construction remain open.
